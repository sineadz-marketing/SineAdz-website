Good to know

A few questions.

What do you need to get started?

A short brief, any brand assets you have and the key message you want people to remember. A rough idea is enough to begin.

Can you create vertical videos for social media?

Yes. We can plan formats for Reels, Shorts, Stories and other placements, including alternate aspect ratios when needed.

Can you animate an existing design?

Often, yes. We first review the source files and decide whether they are suitable for animation or need to be rebuilt.
